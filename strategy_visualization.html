Auction Market Theory Visualized

Explaining the complex algorithmic strategy of OrderFlowKawa through simple visual mechanics, avoiding traditional messy charts.

Value Area (VA) Point of Control (POC)

1. Establishing "Fair Value"

Imagine selling a used car. The algorithm ignores squiggly indicators and just looks at where the money actually traded yesterday.

The Blue Box (Value Area) represents the prices where exactly 70% of all sales happened. This is considered the "Normal Range".

The Orange Line (POC) is the single most popular price agreed upon. The ultimate "Fair Market Value".

The 80% Rule

2. Calling the Market's Bluff

Today, greedy sellers try to open the price crazy high at $22,000 (outside the normal area).

If buyers say "No way" and the price drops back into the Normal Range (Value Area), a mathematical trap is sprung.

The math proves there is an 80% probability the price will completely collapse all the way down to the bottom of the range ($18k) to find balance.

The bot SELLS aggressively to ride the slide down.

Mean Reversion

3. Playing the Range

When the market is boring and trading normally inside the Value Area, the bot acts as a smart negotiator.

When price hits the top ($20k), it's "too expensive". The bot SELLS expecting it to return to the perfect center (POC).

When price hits the bottom ($18k), it's a "steal". The bot BUYS expecting it to bounce back to the perfect center (POC).
